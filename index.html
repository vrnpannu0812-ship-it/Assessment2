Wood-fired kitchen · est. 2019

Everything here starts with fire.

Amber & Ash is a small hearth-cooked kitchen in the old grain store on Kiln Lane. No gas, no electric ovens — just oak, oil, and time.

View the menu Reserve a table

The kitchen

Cooked the slow way, served the same day

We build one oak fire each morning and cook everything from it until it burns down at night — bread, vegetables, whole fish, the lot. The menu is short because the fire only has room for what's good that week.

"It's the kind of place where the smoke gets into your coat and you don't mind at all." — Regional Eating Guide

Good to know

We hold most tables for bookings, but a handful are kept free each night for walk-ins at the counter, first come first served from 5:30pm.

Yes — the menu is marked for vegetarian and gluten-free dishes, and the kitchen is happy to adapt most plates. Let us know when you book.

There's a public car park two minutes' walk on Market Street, and free street parking after 6pm on Kiln Lane itself.

Come as you are. It's a working kitchen with a wood fire going all night — smart casual is about right, and layers help.
